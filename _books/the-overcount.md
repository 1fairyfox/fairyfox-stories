A novella of the Long Quiet, and a new tale in the realm of *The Cartographer of Decks* — it reads
completely on its own. Wren Astel is a teller of the Muster, the office that keeps the *Ark Meridian*
balanced to the body: one aboard for every one the ship can bear, a birth licensed only against a
death. She strikes a beloved name from the register, runs the reconciliation that has never once
failed — and the ship's honest breath comes back high by one. Someone has been aboard her whole life
who was never licensed to draw a single mouthful of this air. Everything she was taught says an
unlicensed body is a leak in a sealed world. Then she has to go and find the face at the end of the
column.

_First person, present tense; taut and procedural, warmth held under. Eleven chapters. The whole
plan — the hidden life, the choice, and the ending — is in the [blueprint](blueprint/)._
