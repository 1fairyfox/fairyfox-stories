### The chapter (~2,400 words, to Idris Okonkwo-Vance's cool-exact voice)

Blueprint ch.3 beats, all landed: training nights; the covenant taught (*drive the route as printed*
— kept precisely *because* no one is watching, "a route, not an audience"); Priya's aptitude shown
(reads the cab and the road like a defect book, doesn't fill silence); **night one `nil`** (the hour
touches nobody — establishes it "does not perform on demand"). Night two the hour comes on with a
witness: the regular **Cyril** (rides for the warm) is taken off the card to the all-night cab office
for company — a **withholding/company** delivery, deliberately unlike ch.1's runaway-*to*-home beat.
Priya sees it clean ("What was that") and **Abel tells his first lie to someone he respects**,
deflecting it as "discretion, kinder this time of year." The book's stated asymmetry is now on the
page and canon: the Vera **"nothing there"** lie was a professional wall that **cost nothing and
kept**; the Priya lie "lands on her," takes a witnessed truth off her, and "tasted of tin."

Then the **Corvus escalation** — the chapter's engine. For the first time in eleven years, 17 does
not just slow at 01:26 but **stops and opens her doors on an empty wet street**, on nobody, idling/
listening outside the door-with-the-light, reaching for the one passenger she has never delivered
(Abel → ch.10) with Priya in the cab. He sits with his hands on nothing, does not rise; she takes the
offer back and drives on. The blue book gains its first new Corvus word: *Slowed. **Opened.** Drove
on.* Priya asks the **Corvus question** (the ledger reserved it for ch.3, not before) — "there's no
stop there and she opened the doors … who does she stop for on Corvus Road?" — and gets the **same
"Nothing there"** he gave Vera, now worse in the dark than it was in daylight. The two lies are
rhymed. Close: end of shift, Priya comes back for gloves and has **the blue notebook open in a steady
grip** ("This isn't the route as printed") — an honest find (she'd clocked its corner since night
one, per ch.2), the direct handoff to ch.4's canteen confession.

Continuity honoured: 23:47 departure, fuel clock 90s fast, Bus 17, the hour's signs (streetlights
half a tone, rain confidential, 17 idling/listening), the fare (say the true thing out loud), the
waiting ones never surprised, Corvus 01:26 walking pace odd side, blue book "four words a night,"
false-log craft. Ledger updated with Ch.3 canon (Cyril, the lie asymmetry to guard, the *Opened*
escalation, the rhymed "Nothing there," Priya holding the book). Efua/Ama/the letter kept unnamed;
Corvus still "a door I have no professional business being aware of" in the narration through ch.3.
